Interface Anatomy Cohort 2

Learn Design and Build Systems In 5 Weeks

Interface Anatomy is a live, cohort-based course for designers who want to go beyond pretty screens and build the systems behind them.

Course Curriculum

Get the Course
Week 1

Systems First: Orientation, Color & Typography

Focus: Understanding design systems before jumping into UI creation.

  • What design systems actually are in real products
  • How this course is structured for practical learning
  • Setting up your Figma workspace for system design
  • Choosing typefaces for systems and understanding hierarchy

Outcome: You build a strong foundation in design systems and set up your workspace with clarity and structure.

Week 2

Space, Structure & Figma Fundamentals

Focus: Building strong foundations in layout, responsiveness and scalable UI systems.

  • Understanding spacing systems and layout logic
  • Using grid systems for web and mobile design
  • Applying responsive design principles
  • Designing for states and interactions

Outcome: You gain the ability to create structured, responsive and scalable UI systems.

Week 3

Strategy, Audits & Style Guides

Focus: Thinking strategically about design systems, audits and documentation.

  • Understanding atomic design and system thinking
  • What an experience audit is and when to use it
  • Difference between a style guide and a design system
  • Planning your system architecture

Outcome: You learn to evaluate products critically and document systems that teams can use.

Week 4

Tokens, Governance & System Maturity

Focus: Scaling, maintaining and operationalizing design systems.

  • What design tokens are and why they matter
  • Understanding primitive, semantic and component tokens
  • Handling developer handoff inside Figma
  • Design systems adoption strategies

Outcome: You learn how to make systems usable, sustainable and impactful inside real teams.

Week 5

AI, Career & Business

Focus: Leveraging AI, building career narratives, and understanding business as a designer.

  • AI assisted workflows for design systems
  • Using AI tools for documentation and tokens
  • Writing effective prompts and evaluating outputs
  • Positioning your portfolio for design system roles

Outcome: You learn how to use AI effectively and think strategically about your growth.
