About Me

Rajat Patel

Instagram LinkedIn

I am Rajat, a design practitioner and mentor with 10+ years of experience across UX, UI, product design, design systems, and front-end aware workflows.

I have designed and shipped real products, built design systems adopted across teams, worked closely with engineers and product leaders, and navigated everything from early-stage chaos to large-scale complexity.

My career has been hands-on by choice. I believe the best mentors are builders first. I do not teach UX as theory. I teach how UX actually works inside real companies, with real constraints, trade-offs, and deadlines.

Why people learn from me

Most UX educators either teach theory without real execution, or they have built things but never learned how to teach clearly. I have done both. I started by designing and coding products end-to-end, then moved into building and leading design systems and product teams at scale.

That range allows me to teach UX, UI, product thinking, and systems as one connected discipline, the way it exists in real life.

When you learn with me, you learn:

  • How to think, not just what to do
  • Why decisions are made, not just how to present them
  • How to work with engineers and product managers effectively
  • How to design for scale, not just screens

My experience, briefly

  • 10+ years in UX, UI, product design, and design systems
  • Built and scaled design systems across multiple organizations
  • Designed zero-to-one products and improved existing ones
  • Worked deeply with engineering and leadership teams
  • Co-author of a granted Indian patent in knowledge systems

Teaching is core to my work

Over the years, I have taught and mentored 2000+ designers through workshops, talks, and online sessions across India.

I also founded India's first Design Systems community. Teaching keeps me sharp. If I cannot explain something clearly, I do not believe I understand it well enough.

That is the bar I hold myself to and the bar I will hold you to.
