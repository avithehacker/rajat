I am Rajat, a design practitioner with 13+ years of experience across UX, UI, product design, design systems, and front-end aware workflows. I have designed and shipped real products, built design systems adopted across teams, and worked closely with engineers and product leaders from early-stage chaos to large-scale complexity.

My career has been hands-on by choice. I believe the best teachers are builders first. I do not teach design as theory — I teach how design actually works inside real companies, with real constraints, trade-offs, and deadlines.

I founded Molecule to give designers a structured place to build real skills — a curriculum built on the work I have actually done.

01 The Approach

Built on practice, not theory

The Molecule curriculum starts from what design work actually looks like inside product companies — ambiguous decisions, engineering constraints, evolving systems, and trade-offs made under real pressure. That is what you practise here.

I started by designing and coding products end-to-end. Then I moved into building and leading design systems at scale. That range lets me teach UX, UI, product thinking, and systems as one connected discipline — the way it exists in real work.

What this means for how you learn

  • You learn how to think, not just what to produce
  • You understand why decisions are made, not just how to present them
  • You practice working with engineers and product managers as collaborators
  • You design for scale and teams — not just for portfolios
  • Every assignment connects to something you would actually do in a job

If I cannot explain something clearly, I do not believe I understand it well enough. That is the bar I hold myself to — and the bar the curriculum reflects.
